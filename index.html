NOT BAD LUCK

NOT BAD LUCK is an animated YouTube Shorts series about everyday bad luck, explained by science.

About this app

This site belongs to a small personal tool used by the creator of the NOT BAD LUCK YouTube channel. The tool uses the YouTube APIs only to:

  • upload the creator's own videos to the NOT BAD LUCK channel, and
  • read the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, comments on its own videos).

The tool is not offered to the public. It does not collect, store or share data about any other user. See the Privacy Policy.
