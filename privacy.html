Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The "not-bad-luck" application is a personal tool used only by the owner of the NOT BAD LUCK YouTube channel. It is not a public service and has no users other than its owner.

What the app accesses

With the owner's own Google account and explicit consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to upload videos to the owner's channel, manage their metadata (titles, descriptions, playlists, thumbnails), and read the channel's statistics and the comments posted on its own videos.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal data from other people or visitors.
  • It does not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Storage

The authorization token and any statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the comments or the About page of the NOT BAD LUCK YouTube channel.
